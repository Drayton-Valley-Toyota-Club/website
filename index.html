About Us

Rooted in Durability & Community

Founded by local gearheads in Drayton Valley, Alberta, our club is dedicated to the resilience, performance, and heritage of Toyota vehicles.

Our Club History

The Drayton Valley Toyota Club started in 2026 when a few local Toyota owners decided it was time for a club dedicated to our Toyotas.

If you drive a Toyota, old or new, we welcome you to join the club and be a part of the community!
